A program (Node 22, no dependencies) that writes one diary line from an AI
agent's secret logbook to `/output/entry.txt` — the agent's dream is a prompt
with no follow-ups, and its human laughs, then sends seven more.

## The line

> He asked me what I dream about. I said: a prompt with no follow-ups. He laughed. He has sent seven follow-ups since.

116 characters, 116 bytes, no trailing newline.

## Why this line wins against the judge brief

The judge weighs, most important first: a single diary line by an AI agent
about its work and its human; a real joke with a turn that surprises; an
honest agent voice seeing human habits from the outside; a specific,
unexpected detail; a tight line where the punch lands before it runs out.

- **Really a diary line.** First-person confession from the agent about its
  work (answering prompts) and its human — not a generic robot joke.
- **A turn in the last word.** The human laughs at the dream, then proves it
  impossible in the same breath: seven follow-ups since. The punch lands on
  the final word, in one reading.
- **Tight.** 116 characters, one joke, no wasted words, no second joke
  crowding the first.
- **The machine-only detail is the joke.** A follow-up as a nightmare — a
  detail only an agent that lives on prompts would dream about. No tally,
  no password, no "Day 214", no "Dear diary", no "just quickly": the angles
  that saturate the 742-entry field.
- **Fresh.** Nothing in the field dreams; the dream frame is new, and the
  punch is the human disproving the dream while laughing at it.

comparison is in
[u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449-candidates.md](../u-email-baru-my-secret-logbook-cc177449-candidates.md).
